How should the schema be written?

Start each question with its number, marks and type, then the question, a model answer and the marking points with their marks:

Q1b [2 marks] [type: descriptive]
Question: What is photosynthesis?
Model answer: Photosynthesis is the process ...
Points:
- Process by which green plants make their own food (1)
- Uses sunlight, CO2 and water to make glucose and oxygen (1)

Q1a [1 mark] [type: mcq]
Question: ... (a) ... (b) ... (c) ... (d) ...
Answer: (b)

Use Q4-OR for an internal choice and [type: manual] for questions you want to mark yourself, such as numericals or diagrams.
