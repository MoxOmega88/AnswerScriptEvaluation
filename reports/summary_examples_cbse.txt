A food chain shows who eats whom in an ecosystem, and energy flows from one level to the next. For example grass is eaten by a deer and the deer is eaten by a lion. The 10 percent law says that only 10 percent of the energy is transferred to the next trophic level. The remaining 90 percent is lost as heat and used for the organism's own activities.
